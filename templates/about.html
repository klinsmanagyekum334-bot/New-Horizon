WHO WE ARE

A household name in Pharmaceuticals & Real Estate

New Horizon Coopers Limited is a company incorporated under the laws of Ghana in 2017 to undertake a variety of businesses including Import and Export, Dealership in Pharmaceutical Products, Real Estate Development and General Construction works — with a vision to become a household name in Pharmaceuticals and Real Estates in Ghana and beyond.

We are a reference in the high-profile Real Estate Consulting sector that addresses international markets and customers from all over the world. Through many years of experience, a team of shrewd professionals with transversal skills has been assembled to lead the Company and accompany our customers in every phase of the process — to establish a beneficial and mutual relationship of trust and transparency.

OUR APPROACH

Every property has its own personality

Each property has its own personality and history of value that must be acknowledged and transferred. Through an in-depth analysis of real estate assets, constant updating on industry scenarios, and assessment of the best investment opportunities — our consultancy is positioned at par with the highest market standard.

Besides competence, it is the ability to create a strong connection with the customer that has always distinguished us. The customers who have chosen us — and continue to do so — know they can count on undisputed confidentiality and on an optimal exploitation of their real estate assets.
